# Hallucination

Hallucination is when an AI says something untrue as if it were a fact.

Imagine a friend who's great at answering questions and telling stories. But when they don't know the answer, instead of saying, "I'm not sure," they make up something that sounds believable, with lots of convincing details, without realizing they're wrong.

The AI isn't lying. Instead, it fills in gaps in what it knows with something that sounds right based on patterns it has learned, even if it is not true.
